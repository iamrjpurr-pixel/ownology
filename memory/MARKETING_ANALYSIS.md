# Ownology · Marketing Analysis
### Where we sit, what's around us, what to say
_Locked 2026-02-07 — for phone-side reference during cold calls_

---

## 1. Category map — where we actually play

Not what you'd assume. Ownology sits in a gap nobody currently owns.

| Category | Players | Fits Ownology? |
|---|---|---|
| **Wine apps** (scanning, ratings, community) | Vivino (65M users, $135M raised), Delectable, CellarTracker | ❌ No scanning, no ratings, no crowd |
| **Wine education** | Wine Folly, WSET, GuildSomm, Court of Master Sommeliers | ❌ Not evergreen articles, not certification-track |
| **Wine media** | Jancis Robinson, Decanter, VinePair, wine.com | ❌ Not editorial |
| **Enterprise winery software** | Vintrace ($500-3k/mo), Ekos, Innovint, VinScape | ❌ Too heavy, no AI, wrong price |
| **AI wine tools (early)** | Preferabli, CleverPour, various ChatGPT wrappers | ⚠️ Closest, but all shallow |
| **Consumer AI Q&A** | Perplexity, ChatGPT | ❌ No persona, no memory, no wine grounding |

**Ownology isn't in any of these boxes.** It sits between them.

---

## 2. The gap we actually occupy

**"AI wine answer engine, grounded in real oenology, with cellar-side infrastructure underneath."**

That's the category nobody's playing in. Two flanks:

- **Consumer flank (DIY):** Wine Folly + Perplexity had a baby. Answer engine, real depth, voice.
- **Professional flank (cellar):** GPT-5 met a cellar hand. Institutional memory, SOP-grounded, opinion-forward.

Same AI infrastructure. Two audiences. Two front doors.

Defensible not because the tech is unique (it isn't), but because the **combination is:** grounding + voice + memory + dual-market backbone.

## 4. Positioning (locked)

**One-liner:**
> **Ownology is the wine answer engine. Grounded in real oenology. Delivered by Owen, the cellar hand who remembers.**

Three positioning pillars:

1. **Grounded, not vibes.** Answers rooted in industry-standard references — not community opinion, not internet consensus, not LLM hallucination.
2. **Direct, not deferential.** Owen has opinions. He'll tell you your $16 Barbera is over-oaked. Cellar-hand honesty, not sommelier flattery.
3. **Yours, compounding.** Every question you ask becomes part of your library. Ask better questions over time.

---

## 5. What to avoid saying

- "AI-powered wine assistant" — generic, sounds like every ChatGPT wrapper
- "Smarter than a sommelier" — antagonises the credentialed community you need on-side
- "The Netflix of wine" — cringe, false story
- Anything about AU wine industry problems in consumer channels
- Anything defensive ("more accurate than X") — proves nothing, invites comparison

---

## 6. Geography

**DIY / consumer marketing = international from day 1.** SEO doesn't respect borders. English-language wine content ranks globally. US + UK are 60% of consumer wine subscription market.

**Pro / winemaker cold-calling = AU first.** Rich's network is there, first 3 pilots come from there, contracting market means small-batch AU wineries need this specifically. Case studies produced become internationally useful for later NZ/US expansion.

---

## 7. The strongest moat (that's not yet obvious)

**Owen's persona + accumulated `/ask` answer corpus.**

Every published `/ask` page is a permanent asset. After 200 pages published in a distinctive voice, that voice becomes uncopyable — a competitor could ship the same tech in 3 months but couldn't replicate 200 pages of Owen's writing overnight.

**Content is the moat. AI is the substrate.**

Marketing math: every hour writing a great `/ask` answer buys a permanent asset. Every hour on paid ads evaporates when the ads stop.

## 8. Cold-call talking points (memorise these two)

**When a winemaker asks "what makes you different from Vivino / Wine Folly / ChatGPT?"**

> "Vivino tells you what the crowd thinks about a bottle. Wine Folly teaches you evergreen wine concepts. ChatGPT will guess at wine questions with confidence but no depth. Ownology sits somewhere else — it's the answer engine grounded in real oenology, and on the professional side, it's a cellar hand that remembers your last three vintages. Different job. Different tool."

**When a winemaker asks "why now?"**

> "Because the AU wine industry is contracting — you know that better than me — and the wineries that survive the shakeout are the ones running lean with tools that actually save time. Ownology's designed to be the one tool you don't cut when things get tight."
