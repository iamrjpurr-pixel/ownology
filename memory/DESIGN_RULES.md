# Ownology · Design Rules
### Non-negotiable rules for every marketing page. Locked 2026-02-07.

These three rules came from a Human Factors Engineering review of Ownology's positioning. They apply to every landing page, marketing email, cold-call script, and product screen from here on.

---

## Rule 1 — Show the demo, always

**Every marketing page must show Owen answering a real wine question above the fold, right next to the marketing phrase.**

Why: "Wine Answer Engine" means nothing on its own. The moment a buyer sees a real Owen answer next to those words, the story flips from "sounds cool" to "oh, it actually works."

Example — do this:

```
OWNOLOGY
The Wine Answer Engine.

Try one now:
> What does Lagrein taste like?
Owen: Deep, dark, chocolatey — like a brooding Shiraz with menthol
and blue fruits. Traditionally from far north Italy…
```

Never ship the phrase alone. Always with a live example.

---

## Rule 2 — Never name a competitor

**No page, email, cold-call script, or marketing surface may name Vivino, Wine Folly, ChatGPT, Vintrace, Ekos, Delectable, or any other product by name.**

Why: naming a competitor plants them in the buyer's head. They'll open a new tab and Google them. You may never see them again. Confident brands don't punch sideways.

Do this instead:

- **Say what you ARE** — "grounded answers rooted in real oenology"
- **Describe the category** — "the big wine-scanner apps" (implies Vivino without naming)
- **Let the contrast be implicit** — the buyer will draw the comparison themselves

Rule of thumb: if a competitor's name would appear in the transcript, rewrite the sentence.

---

## Rule 3 — Every Owen answer gets the disclosure line

**At the bottom of every Owen answer, always show:**

> Owen is Ownology's AI — grounded, but not perfect.
> For medical or business decisions, verify with a human expert.

Why:
1. Legal/regulator safety — protects against liability if someone acts on bad advice
2. Trust — winemakers and wine-savvy consumers respect a product that admits its limits
3. Differentiation — every competitor over-claims; under-claiming reads as radically honest

Small line. Big trust return.

---

## The design law that generated all three rules

> Every marketing surface must show BOTH what Ownology is AND what it does, in one eye-scan. No abstract positioning without a concrete demo. No claim without a disclosure. No comparison without a positive definition.

If a new page fails any of these three tests, it doesn't ship.
